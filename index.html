Privacy Policy

Peaceful Solitaire · Last updated 12 October 2026

In short

Peaceful Solitaire does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal information. The game runs entirely on your device and never connects to the internet.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code that collects data.

What is saved on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, the game keeps the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • Your settings: theme, card back, sound and haptics choices, draw mode and layout.
  • Your current game and its undo history.
  • Your play statistics: games played and won, win streaks, best time and fewest moves.
  • Your achievements and daily-ritual history.
  • A shuffled deal prepared for your next game.

This information never leaves your device and is not available to other apps.

Permissions

The Android app asks for one permission: vibration, used to give a small tap when a card lands. It does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or any other personal data.

Third parties

None. The game does not load fonts, images, scripts, ads or any other content from the internet, and it contains no third-party analytics or advertising tools.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no advertising, in-app purchases, chat or social features.

Deleting your data

Everything the game saves stays on your device. To erase it, open Android Settings, choose Apps, then Peaceful Solitaire, then Storage, and tap Clear data. Uninstalling the app removes it as well.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date at the top.
